# Vibes Nest Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 16 November 2026_

Vibes Nest ("the app") is a personal wellbeing app built by Sasmitha Tharushan.
This policy explains what data the app collects, why, and how you control it.
In short: **your data is used only to run the app for you. It is never sold or
used for advertising.**

## What we collect

| Data | Why | Who can see it |
|---|---|---|
| Name and email address | To create your account and greet you | Only you |
| Mood check-ins, sleep logs, journal notes, tasks | To show your history and progress | Only you |
| "Talk to Nest" chat messages | To continue your conversation | Only you |
| My Garden points and streaks | To show your progress | Only you |
| Community posts | To share with the community | All signed-in users, under an anonymous nickname. Your name and email are never shown |
| Hugs and reports you give | To count them fairly (one per person) | Only you; others only see the totals |

The app does **not** collect your location, contacts, photos or advertising ID.

## Where your data is stored

Data is stored with **Google Firebase** (Authentication and Cloud Firestore),
in Google Cloud. Security rules ensure only you can read your private data.
Firestore also keeps a cached copy on your device so the app works offline.

## AI companion ("Talk to Nest")

Messages you type to Nest, together with recent messages from the same
conversation, are sent to **Google's Gemini model through Firebase AI Logic**
to generate a reply. Please don't share information you aren't comfortable
sending to this service.

Messages that suggest a crisis (for example, thoughts of self-harm) are
**detected on your device and are not sent to the AI**. Instead, the app shows
helplines straight away.

Nest is an AI, not a therapist or doctor, and cannot provide medical advice.

## App integrity

The app uses **Firebase App Check** (Google Play Integrity) to confirm requests
come from the genuine app. This helps protect the service from abuse.

## Your choices

- **Delete content:** you can delete journal notes, sleep logs, tasks, your
  chat history and your community posts at any time in the app.
- **Delete your account:** Profile → Delete account permanently removes your
  account and all of your data listed above, including your community posts.
- **Export or questions:** open an issue at
  https://github.com/SasmithaTharushan/vibes_nest/issues.

## Children

Vibes Nest is not intended for children under 13. If you are under 18,
please use the app with the support of a parent, guardian or trusted adult.

## In a crisis

Vibes Nest is not an emergency service. In Sri Lanka, call **1926** (National
Mental Health Helpline, 24/7) or **1333** (CCCline, 24/7). In immediate danger,
call **119**.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the "Last updated" date above will change and the app
will link to the new version.
